# Bundled pronunciation audio

`a.mp3`, `i.mp3`, `u.mp3`, `e.mp3` and `o.mp3` contain one synthesized Japanese mora each (あ, い, う, え and お). The `ka.mp3` through `ko.mp3` clips contain the K row (か, き, く, け and こ). The remaining clips cover the rest of the 46 basic hiragana. The clip for を uses its usual pronunciation, o. Additional clips cover dakuten, handakuten, and contracted hiragana sounds. ぢ and づ share the clips for じ and ず. Katakana shares the same clips for matching sounds. The sound rules guide adds six word clips, and everyday vocabulary adds eight, with the same voice. The weekday kanji lessons have seven word clips. The listening passages have normal and 0.8× recordings. The katakana loanword reference adds 13 full-word examples. All clips are bundled for offline playback.

NihongoLearn generated the clips with Open JTalk (`pyopenjtalk-plus` 0.4.1.post10) and HTS Voice “Mei”, released by the MMDAgent Project Team. Kana use the default `mei_normal` voice at 0.85× speaking speed. The みゃ and びょ clips use the two target phonemes from contextual labels because isolated synthesis collapsed them into ま and ぼ. The resulting phoneme was checked against the expected phonemes. The output was trimmed, normalized, and encoded as mono 44.1 kHz MP3 at 96 kb/s. These are synthesized examples and await human pronunciation review.
